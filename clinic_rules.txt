# Sunrise Community Clinic: visit rules (made-up practice data)

## Late arrival
If you are late for a session you registered for, the rule depends on how late you are. Up to 10 minutes late: we keep your number and you are called in your turn. 10 to 20 minutes late: your number is moved to the end of the queue for that session. More than 20 minutes late: your number is cancelled, and you can register again at the front desk if the session still has room. Exception: children under 12 and seniors aged 65 or over keep their place for up to 20 minutes. If you know you will be late, please phone the front desk before the session starts.

## Fever or cough symptoms
If you have a fever or a cough, please put on a mask before you enter the clinic and tell the front desk when you register. You will wait in the separate waiting area next to the entrance, and you will be called in your turn. If the mask is a problem, for example for a small baby, tell the staff and they will find a quiet place for you to wait. This is a clinic procedure, not medical advice. The online assistant cannot say what the symptoms mean.

## Pediatric visit
For every pediatrics visit, please bring the child's health booklet (兒童健康手冊) and the child's NHI card. Babies under 3 months are called first in the pediatrics session: tell the front desk when you arrive. A parent or guardian must stay with the child. Exception: a child aged 12 to 17 may come with a written note from a parent, but only for a repeat visit with the same doctor.

## Wheelchair and mobility access
The clinic is on the ground floor and has a ramp at the entrance. Patients who use a wheelchair or a walking aid are called first if they register at least 10 minutes before the session starts. A mobility scooter longer than 1 metre cannot enter the waiting room: please park it in front of the clinic, and staff will help you inside. Please tell the front desk if you need help.

## Medical records and certificates
A copy of your medical records costs NT$100 and is ready in 3 working days. Bring your photo ID. If someone else collects it for you, they need your written permission and both photo IDs. A diagnosis certificate (診斷證明書) costs NT$150 and can be issued the same day, but only if you saw the doctor in the last 3 months. If it was longer ago, you need to register and see the doctor again.

## Referral letter
A referral letter (轉診單) is written by the doctor during your visit, if the doctor decides it is needed. The front desk cannot issue a referral letter without a visit. The letter is valid for 10 days. If you want a referral for a problem you did not discuss in your visit, you need to register again.

## Continuous prescription for chronic conditions
For a stable chronic condition, the doctor may give a continuous prescription (慢性病連續處方箋) that can be filled up to 3 times, about 30 days each. This is available only in family medicine and internal medicine, and only if the doctor decides it. You cannot collect the next refill earlier than 10 days before the due date. You need to bring your NHI card each time. If your condition changes, you need to see the doctor again.

## Lost and found
Items found in the clinic are kept at the front desk for 30 days. To claim an item, show your photo ID and describe the item. NHI cards are kept for 14 days, then handed to the nearest NHI office. After 30 days, other items are given to charity. Phones, wallets and other valuables must be collected in person.

## Privacy and photos
Photography and recording are not allowed in the consultation rooms. A family member may enter the consultation room with the patient only if the patient agrees. The clinic does not share an adult patient's records with relatives without the patient's written permission. Exception: in an emergency, the doctor may speak to the family.

## Typhoon and bad weather closures
When Kaohsiung City announces a typhoon day off, the clinic is closed. We record the decision on the front desk phone message by 07:30. Appointments for that day are cancelled without any penalty, and a missed session does not count as a no-show. Please register again for the next open session. For heavy rain without an official day off, the clinic stays open at the normal hours.
